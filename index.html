Engineering Excellence Since 2015

Founded in 2015, Avant Steelfab Pvt Ltd. has a singular vision: to be a leader in providing world-class steel fabrication solutions. This mission is supported by our sister company, Theeran & Co., which has been delivering exceptional service since 2012. Together, we form a formidable force in the steel fabrication industry.
Our relentless commitment to quality, precision engineering, and adopting advanced technology has been the driving force behind our rapid growth. Today, our team of over 200 highly skilled employees operates from modern facilities equipped with state-of-the-art machinery and cutting-edge design tools. This powerful combination allows us to deliver superior products and services.
As a premier provider, we specialize in the design, manufacturing, and installation of structural and custom steel products. We leverage industry-leading software like SolidWorks and AutoCAD to ensure unparalleled accuracy and innovation from the initial design phase through to final engineering. Our expertise allows us to cater to the complex and diverse needs of construction, industrial, and infrastructure projects.

Services

Pre-Engineered Buildings

Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEBs) in steel fabrication are structures built from factory-made, precision-cut steel components (like columns, beams, purlins, and cladding) that are bolted together on-site, offering a cost-effective, rapid, and flexible alternative to conventional construction, ideal for warehouses, hangars, and industrial facilities.

Heavy Interior Fabrication

Heavy interior fabrication involves the design, creation, and installation of durable, often structural, components within interior spaces, frequently utilizing materials like mild steel, stainless steel, aluminum, glass, and wood.
