Services

Pre-Engineered Buildings

Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEBs) in steel fabrication are structures built from factory-made, precision-cut steel components (like columns, beams, purlins, and cladding) that are bolted together on-site, offering a cost-effective, rapid, and flexible alternative to conventional construction, ideal for warehouses, hangars, and industrial facilities.

Heavy Interior Fabrication

Heavy interior fabrication involves the design, creation, and installation of durable, often structural, components within interior spaces, frequently utilizing materials like mild steel, stainless steel, aluminum, glass, and wood.
